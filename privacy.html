NGIS Offline Maps privacy notice

Beta · last updated 17 November 2026

NGIS Offline Maps is a map, place directory and route planner for Ghana that works without mobile data. This notice says what the app keeps on your phone and what it sends to us.

Kept on your phone

  • Where you are. The map and routes use it, and it stays on the phone unless you choose to share road speeds (below).
  • What you search for, your recent places, home, work and favourites.
  • The maps you download.

Sent to us

  • Reports you choose to send from "Suggest an edit": what you wrote and where the place is.
  • App health: how long the app was open, whether it crashed, and errors, with the versions of the app, of Android or iOS, and of the map. It helps us find and fix crashes. It's sent only after you've seen this notice in the app.
  • Road speeds, only if you choose to share them. While you navigate by car or okada, how long each piece of road of about 200 metres took, where it is (to about 10 metres), which way and when (to the minute). They leave out the first and last 300 metres of every trip. They make everyone's travel times count the traffic. The app asks the first time you navigate by car or okada, and the switch is under Privacy on the Regions screen.
  • Downloads of maps and of places' photos, and checks for map updates and for traffic. They ask our server for files, as any download does.

Reports and app health carry a random number made on your phone, so we can tell they came from the same phone. There's no account, and we don't collect your name, phone number or location. Road speeds don't carry the random number, so they can't be linked to your phone, or to each other beyond one trip. We don't sell data or show ads.

Removing it

Uninstalling the app removes everything it kept on your phone. To have what it sent removed, send a report from the app saying so, or write to christian.dziwornu@milliardienergo.com. The random number lets us find it. Road speeds have no number to find them by; turning sharing off drops those not sent yet.

Contact

NGIS Offline Maps is made by Novaya Geo Information Systems. For questions about your data, or to ask for it to be removed, write to christian.dziwornu@milliardienergo.com.
